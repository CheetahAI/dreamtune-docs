The agent funding flow creates a purchase of USD-denominated Dreamtune credit.
Your agent's wallet signs the crypto transfer. Dreamtune supplies payment
instructions and verifies settlement; it never needs a seed phrase or private key.

## Before you start

Use a workspace key with explicit `billing:read` and `billing:topup` permissions.
Existing keys do not gain billing access automatically. Model, tool and compute
permissions remain separate. A zero balance does not prevent requesting a top-up.

Configure your wallet's transfer limits and allowed assets/networks independently.
The Dreamtune key does not grant permission to move funds from that wallet.

## Request and pay

1. Read `GET /v1/developer/billing/balance`.
2. Discover enabled assets with `GET /v1/developer/billing/crypto/options`.
3. Select an asset `code` and request options again with `?currency=<code>` to
   obtain its current gateway minimum. Limits are USD cents.
4. Save an idempotency key, then create a top-up with a decimal USD `amount` and
   the selected `payCurrency`. Reuse that key only with identical parameters.
5. Transfer only when `instructionsState` is `ready` and the instructions have
   not expired. Verify the exact asset, network, address, amount and required memo.
6. Refresh the saved top-up until `state` is `credited` before spending the credit.

See the [billing endpoint reference](/api/billing) for routes, fields and errors.
The requested USD amount is wallet credit. The platform fee is included in the
payment price; gateway and sending-network charges may also apply. They do not
increase the purchased credit. Asset/network minimums can change.

## Use MCP

The hosted connection will expose `wallet_balance`, `crypto_top_up_options`,
`create_crypto_top_up`, `get_crypto_top_up` and `check_crypto_top_up` after rollout.
Use [the MCP connection guide](/connect/mcp) with your scoped workspace key.
These tools request and track a payment. Your separate wallet integration
authorizes, signs and broadcasts the transfer.

## Recover an uncertain request

Read the saved top-up or repeat identical creation input with its original
idempotency key. Do not generate another intent just because a response was lost.
`creating` and `needs_reconciliation` instructions are not permission to transfer.
An expired quote must not be paid or silently refreshed.

`ready` means the purchase was created, not that it was paid. Only `credited`
establishes funding. A client-supplied transaction hash or checkout redirect does
not prove settlement. Partial, repeated or wrong-asset deposits may need support
review. Never reuse an old deposit address for a new purchase.

Before enabling unattended replenishment, complete a controlled funding test,
verify the resulting balance and inspect the next authorized run's charge.
